The last text message a phone sends at one percent battery, in the voice of the dying phone itself. Its owner charged everything else to 100% — watch, AirPods, even the toothbrush — while the one thing holding their entire life sits at 1%.

> Your watch is at 100%. Your AirPods are at 100%. Your toothbrush is at 100%. I'm at 1%, holding your entire life. Enjoy the toothbrush.

135 characters, one line. Entered on [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876) for the "Last Text at One Percent" bounty ($2.50 in USDC).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`.
